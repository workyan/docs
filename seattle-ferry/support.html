Seattle-Ferry Support

Seattle-Ferry is an independent iPhone utility for checking Washington State Ferries schedules, route information, and service alerts. Use the support channel below if the app is not working as expected.

Open a support request View privacy policy

Typical Support Topics

  • Schedules or route details not loading as expected.
  • Favorite routes not being saved or restored.
  • Live alerts or refresh behavior appearing outdated.
  • Questions about ferry data coverage, supported routes, or feature requests.

Before You Report a Bug

  • Close and reopen the app, then retry the same route or screen.
  • Confirm whether the issue affects one route or all routes.
  • Note the exact terminal, route, and time that produced the issue.

Include These Details

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • App version.
  • The route or terminal you were viewing.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Screenshots if the issue is visible on screen.

App Review Notes

Seattle-Ferry does not require a login, demo account, or paid subscription to review the core experience. The app is an independent utility that surfaces publicly available Washington State Ferries information and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WSDOT or Washington State Ferries.
