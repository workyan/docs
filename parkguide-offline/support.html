ParkGuide-Offline Support

ParkGuide-Offline is a paid iPhone app focused on offline-ready park guides, simple trip prep, and quick access to practical visitor information when you do not have a reliable connection.

Open a support request View privacy policy

Common Support Topics

  • Offline park pack download or storage issues.
  • Park content not appearing after download.
  • Favorites, saved parks, or checklist items not behaving as expected.
  • Suggestions for new parks, better offline details, or trip-planning improvements.

Before You Contact Support

  • Close and reopen the app, then retry the same action.
  • Check whether the issue happens on one park pack or all park packs.
  • Confirm available device storage if a download appears incomplete.

Include These Details

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • App version.
  • The park involved, if the issue is park-specific.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots if the problem is visible on screen.

Response Target

Support requests are typically reviewed within 3 business days.
