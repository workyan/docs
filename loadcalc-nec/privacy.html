LoadCalc-NEC Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

LoadCalc-NEC is an iPhone utility for NEC-oriented electrical load calculations. This privacy policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

LoadCalc-NEC does not require account creation, does not use advertising trackers, and is designed so saved calculations, inputs, and preferences remain on your device.

Information the app handles

When you use LoadCalc-NEC, you may enter calculation values such as service load inputs, dwelling details, feeder values, branch circuit values, demand assumptions, reference selections, saved job names, and app preferences. The app may also store recent calculations and your last-used settings for convenience.

How information is used

This information is used only to perform electrical load calculations, display results, restore your preferences, and help you reopen previously saved local jobs on the same device.

Data storage

LoadCalc-NEC is designed for offline use. Calculation inputs, saved jobs, recent history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. The current version does not provide account registration, cloud sync, or a remote project database.

What LoadCalc-NEC does not do

  • Does not require login for core features.
  • Does not sell your data.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Does not include third-party ad SDKs.
  • Does not collect payment card details inside the app.

Third-party services

The app may rely on standard Apple platform services that support installation, App Store purchase validation, and user-initiated share actions. If a future version adds analytics, cloud backup, sign-in, or other external integrations, this policy will be updated before those changes are introduced.

Your choices

You can clear saved values and local calculation history from within the app where supported, or remove local app data by deleting the app from your device.

Children's privacy

LoadCalc-NEC is a technical utility and is not directed to children.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
