RoofPitch-Calc Support

This page is provided for the App Store support link and review reference.

RoofPitch-Calc is a fast iPhone roof math utility for pitch conversion, slope angle checks, rafter length estimates, roof area calculations, and quick reference lookups.

Get support

If the app is not working as expected, use the developer contact information shown on the App Store listing. You can also open a support request in the shared documentation repository.

Open a support request

When reporting a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful when a result, layout, or unit conversion looks incorrect.

Frequently asked questions

Does RoofPitch-Calc require an account?
No. The app is designed to work without login.

Does RoofPitch-Calc upload my measurements?
No. The current version is designed for offline use and stores recent calculations and presets on your device.

Can I use RoofPitch-Calc on a jobsite without signal?
Yes. Core calculations are intended to work offline.

What can the app calculate?
It can calculate pitch ratio, roof angle, slope percentage, rafter length, approximate roof area, and squares estimates from basic roof dimensions.
