What the App Does

Golf-Stroke is a focused golf scorekeeping utility for iPhone and Apple Watch. You can start a 9-hole or 18-hole round, track strokes and penalties, review hole-by-hole results, and keep round history on-device.

Common Questions

Do I need an account?
No. The current version does not require sign-in or a test account.

Does the app upload my round data?
No. The current version stores round history locally on your device and does not rely on a developer-operated backend.

Does the app include GPS or official rules certification?
No. Golf-Stroke is a simple scorekeeper and does not provide GPS distance, tournament certification, or official rules rulings.
