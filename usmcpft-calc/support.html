USMCPFT-Calc Support

This page is provided for the App Store support link and review reference.

USMCPFT-Calc is a fast iPhone utility for estimating 2026 Marine Physical Fitness Test scores. Enter pull-up or push-up results, plank time, and 3-mile run time to see total score, event breakdown, and pass status in one place.

USMCPFT-Calc is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with the U.S. Marine Corps. For formal evaluation or current policy questions, always verify the latest official requirements separately.

Frequently asked questions

Does USMCPFT-Calc require an account?
No. The app is designed to work without login.

Does USMCPFT-Calc upload my scores?
No. The current version is designed for offline use and stores recent results and preferences on your device.

Can I use USMCPFT-Calc without network access?
Yes. Core score calculation and local reference use are intended to work offline.

Does the app include official USMC policy?
No. It is an unofficial calculator for quick reference and training use.
