DeployPay-US Privacy Policy

DeployPay-US is an iPhone app that helps users estimate U.S. military pay, deployment pay, BAH, BAS, and drill pay. The app is designed to keep data collection minimal and to perform calculations for informational use only.

1. Information the App Uses

  • Calculator inputs you enter, such as branch, pay grade, years of service, and location details.
  • App preferences and recent scenarios saved for convenience on your device.
  • Bundled pay tables and lookup data included in the app package.

2. How the Information Is Used

  • To calculate estimated monthly pay, deployment pay, BAH, BAS, and drill pay.
  • To save recent scenarios and preferences locally so the app is faster to reuse.
  • To provide troubleshooting context if you contact support and choose to share details.

3. Data Storage

  • DeployPay-US is intended to store app preferences and saved scenarios on your device.
  • DeployPay-US does not require account creation.
  • DeployPay-US does not include public profiles, social posting, or in-app messaging.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share calculator entries with advertisers.
  • We do not claim affiliation with the U.S. Department of Defense or any military branch.

5. Data Accuracy and Sources

DeployPay-US provides estimates based on bundled pay tables, allowance data, and calculation rules. Results are not official government outputs and should be verified with current official sources before making financial decisions.

6. Children's Privacy

DeployPay-US is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, contact support using the support page.

7. Policy Changes

If this policy changes materially, this page will be updated and the effective date will be revised.
