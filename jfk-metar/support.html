JFK-METAR Support

JFK-METAR is a focused weather utility for checking current JFK / KJFK METAR, TAF, decoded conditions, and widgets. If the app is not working as expected, use the links below to request support.

Open a support request View privacy policy

Common Questions

  • Weather looks outdated: reopen the app and confirm the source has published a newer report.
  • Widget not updating: verify the widget is active and allow iOS time to refresh the timeline.
  • Decoded values look unexpected: compare the raw METAR and observation timestamp shown in the app.
  • TAF or flight category differs from expectation: source data may have changed or a new report may be pending.

Support Covers

  • App launch or stability problems
  • JFK weather refresh and display issues
  • Widget setup or refresh questions
  • Privacy-related questions

Include These Details

  • Device model and iOS version
  • App version
  • What you expected to see
  • What the app showed instead
  • Screenshots if the issue is visible on screen

Data Attribution and Disclaimer

Weather data is sourced from public aviation weather interfaces. JFK-METAR is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by JFK Airport, FAA, NOAA, the National Weather Service, or the Port Authority.
