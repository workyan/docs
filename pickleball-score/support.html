Pickleball-Score Support

Pickleball-Score is an iPhone-first scorekeeping utility focused on keeping pickleball matches moving with less friction. If the app is not working as expected, use the support channel below.

Open a support request View privacy policy

Typical Support Topics

  • Scoring display confusion or unexpected score changes.
  • Restoring defaults or clearing local match history.
  • Bug reports related to match setup, serve flow, or live scoring.
  • Feature requests for doubles, singles, or scorekeeping shortcuts.

Before You Report a Bug

  • Close and reopen the app, then retry the same flow.
  • Confirm the exact steps that led to the issue.
  • Check whether the issue happens every time or only in one match setup.

Include These Details

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • App version.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Screenshots if the problem is visible on screen.

Response Target

Support requests are typically reviewed within 3 business days.
