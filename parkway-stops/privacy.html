Privacy Policy

Parkway Stops is designed to help you explore the Blue Ridge Parkway without creating an account or building a profile about you.

Effective date · July 17, 2026

What we collect

Parkway Stops does not collect personal information. You can use the app without an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps its bundled guide data, your saved favorites, and your app preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to make the app work and is not sent to our servers.

Location and navigation

Parkway Stops does not track your location. When you choose to navigate to a stop, the app may hand the stop's coordinates to Apple Maps. Apple Maps is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

We do not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics trackers, or a mandatory account backend. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information because we do not collect it.

Children's privacy

Parkway Stops is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and revise its effective date before those changes take effect.

Contact

For a privacy question, please open a support request or visit the Parkway Stops support page.

Parkway Stops is an independent guide utility. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Park Service or the Blue Ridge Parkway.
