TouchWave-Remote Support

This page is intended for App Store support and review reference.

About the app

TouchWave-Remote is a paid iPhone utility that helps users discover and control compatible SoundTouch speakers on the same Wi-Fi network. The app focuses on quick speaker selection, playback controls, volume controls, mute, preset shortcuts, and manual IP fallback.

Common questions

Why can't the app find my speaker?
Make sure your iPhone and speaker are on the same Wi-Fi network and that Local Network access is allowed for the app in iOS Settings.

Can I add a speaker manually?
Yes. If automatic discovery does not work in your setup, use manual IP entry when available in the app.

Does the app require an account or subscription?
No. The app is designed as a one-time purchase utility with no account login and no subscription.

Does the app work with every speaker model?
The app is built for compatible SoundTouch speakers, but compatibility may vary by model, firmware version, and local network conditions.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm the speaker is powered on and already connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your iPhone.
  • Check Local Network permission in iOS Settings.
  • If discovery fails, try manual IP entry.
  • If commands do not respond, verify that the selected speaker is the device currently shown in the app.

Feedback

If you need help, use the developer contact information shown on the App Store listing. When reporting an issue, include your device model, iOS version, app version, speaker model, and steps to reproduce the problem.

Review notes

This app does not require a test account or login. Core functionality is based on local network speaker discovery and direct control of compatible devices on the same Wi-Fi network.
