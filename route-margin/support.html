Common questions

Quick answers.

Does Route-Margin require an account?

No. Route-Margin works without registration or login.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Core calculations and saved history work offline. Route-Margin does not need a remote service to calculate an estimate.

Does the app upload my load details?

No. Your vehicle profile and recent estimates are stored locally on your device. A summary leaves the app only when you deliberately use the iOS share sheet and choose a destination.

How is estimated profit calculated?

Route-Margin subtracts estimated fuel, variable mileage costs, fees, tolls, and other entered costs from gross pay. Results depend entirely on your inputs and assumptions.

Does Route-Margin provide financial or legal advice?

No. Results are estimates for planning purposes. Route-Margin is not a tax, payroll, legal, ELD, dispatch, navigation, load-board, or fuel-price service. Confirm settlement terms and operating costs independently.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete estimates, clear history, or clear all local data from the app's settings. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.
