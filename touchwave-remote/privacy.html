TouchWave-Remote Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

TouchWave-Remote is an independent iPhone remote for compatible SoundTouch speakers on a local Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what information the app handles, where that information is stored, and how you can manage it.

Summary: the current version does not require an account, does not run ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not perform cross-app tracking. Speaker settings and app preferences are stored on your device.

Information the app may handle

To discover and control compatible speakers, the app may process the following information on your device:

  • Local network device details, such as speaker name, local IP address, and connection state.
  • Manual speaker entries you add, such as IP addresses or friendly names.
  • App preferences, such as a default speaker, preset labels, and basic interface settings.

How information is used

This information is used only to help the app discover speakers on your Wi-Fi network, send remote control commands, remember your local setup, and restore your preferred experience the next time you open the app.

Where data is stored

TouchWave-Remote is designed as a local-first utility. The current version does not provide account registration, developer-hosted cloud sync, or an app backend that stores your speaker list or preferences. Data described above is stored locally on your device.

Permissions

The app may request Local Network permission on iOS so it can discover compatible speakers and communicate with them on the same Wi-Fi network. If you deny this permission, automatic discovery may not work. You may still be able to use manual IP entry if supported by the app version you install.

What we do not do

  • We do not require account creation or sign-in.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use third-party analytics in the current version.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not ask for contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access for the core remote features.

Compatibility notice

TouchWave-Remote is an independent utility for compatible speakers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Bose Corporation or any official SoundTouch product team. Product names are used only to describe compatibility.

Your choices

You can remove manually added speaker information, change saved preferences, or uninstall the app. When the app is removed, locally stored app data is typically removed by the operating system as well.

Children's privacy

This app is a speaker remote utility and is not designed as a service for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Policy changes

If future versions add accounts, cloud features, analytics, advertising, or other data flows, this policy will be updated before or when those changes are released.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the App Store listing or visit the support page.
