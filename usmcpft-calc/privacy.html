USMCPFT-Calc Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

USMCPFT-Calc is an iPhone app that helps users estimate 2026 Marine Physical Fitness Test scores. This privacy policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

USMCPFT-Calc is an unofficial tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or published by the U.S. Marine Corps.

USMCPFT-Calc does not require account creation, does not include advertising trackers, and is designed so your recent results, inputs, and preferences remain on your device.

Information the app handles

When you use USMCPFT-Calc, you may enter age group, sex, pull-up or push-up counts, plank time, 3-mile run time, saved results, and app preferences. The app may store recent calculations and your last-used settings for convenience.

How information is used

This information is used only to calculate scores, display event breakdowns, show pass or fail status, restore your preferences, and help you review recent local results on the same device.

Data storage

USMCPFT-Calc is designed for offline use. Score inputs, recent history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. The current version does not provide account registration, cloud sync, or a remote user database.

What USMCPFT-Calc does not do

  • Does not require login for core features.
  • Does not sell your data.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Does not include third-party ad SDKs.
  • Does not collect payment card details inside the app.

Third-party services

The app may rely on standard Apple platform services that support installation, App Store purchase processing, and user-initiated share actions. If a future version adds analytics, backup, sign-in, or other external integrations, this policy will be updated before those changes are introduced.

Your choices

You can clear saved local results from within the app where supported, or remove local app data by deleting the app from your device.

Children's privacy

USMCPFT-Calc is a fitness utility and is not directed to children.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
