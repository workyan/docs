Seller-Fee Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

Seller-Fee is a fee and profit calculator for marketplace sellers. This policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

Seller-Fee does not require account creation, does not use advertising trackers, and is designed so calculations and saved history stay on your device.

Information the app handles

When you use Seller-Fee, you may enter pricing and calculation data such as item price, shipping charged, shipping cost, item cost, quantity, discounts, fee settings, target profit values, and saved calculation presets.

How information is used

This information is used only to perform fee calculations, show profit and margin results, store your recent history, and restore your saved settings on the same device.

Data storage

Seller-Fee is designed for offline use. Calculation data, history, and settings are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide account registration or cloud sync in the current version.

What Seller-Fee does not do

  • Does not collect personal accounts or passwords.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Does not sell your data.
  • Does not use third-party ad SDKs.
  • Does not require login to use core features.

Third-party services

Seller-Fee is not affiliated with Etsy or any marketplace platform. The app is a standalone calculator tool. If a future release adds analytics, cloud backup, or other third-party integrations, this policy will be updated before those changes are introduced.

Your choices

You can delete saved values and recent calculation history from within the app by clearing app data or removing the app from your device.

Children's privacy

Seller-Fee is a business utility app and is not directed to children.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
