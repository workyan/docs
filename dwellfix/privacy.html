DwellFix Privacy Policy

DwellFix is a home maintenance checklist app for iPhone. The app is designed to work primarily on your device and to keep data collection minimal.

1. Information DwellFix Uses

  • Task data you create or edit, such as maintenance items, schedules, completion dates, and notes.
  • Basic app settings and preferences stored locally on your device.
  • Home profile details you choose to enter, such as property type or maintenance-related setup options.

2. How That Information Is Used

  • To show your maintenance checklist, due dates, upcoming items, and overdue items.
  • To store your completion history and custom tasks.
  • To personalize starter task suggestions based on the home details you provide.

3. Data Storage

  • DwellFix is intended to store its core checklist data on-device.
  • DwellFix does not require account creation.
  • DwellFix does not provide public posting, social profiles, or community features.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your task data with advertisers.
  • We do not knowingly collect analytics, account data, or cloud profile data for the current app version described here.

5. Third-Party Services

If you choose to contact support through GitHub Issues, the information you submit there is handled by GitHub under GitHub's own terms and privacy practices.

6. Children's Privacy

DwellFix is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe inappropriate information was submitted, contact us through the support page.

7. Policy Changes

If this policy changes materially, this page will be updated and the effective date will be revised.
