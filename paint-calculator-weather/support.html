Quick checks

Start here.

01

A calculation looks wrong

Check the unit system, paint coverage, number of coats, waste buffer, and whether doors or windows were subtracted. Manufacturer coverage varies by paint and surface.

02

A project is missing

Projects are saved only on your device. They do not sync through an account and may be removed when the app is deleted.

03

Weather will not load

Check your internet connection and try again. If current location is unavailable, enter a US city, state, or ZIP code manually. You can review location access in iOS Settings.

For faster help

What to include.

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version shown in Settings
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • The exact error message, if one appeared
  • Steps that reliably reproduce the issue

Please do not post precise location coordinates, private project details, or other sensitive information in a public request.

Weather & paint guidance

A planning tool, not a guarantee.

Forecasts change, and actual surface conditions can differ from reported weather. Always follow the paint manufacturer's label and technical data sheet, inspect the surface, and use appropriate professional judgment before painting.
