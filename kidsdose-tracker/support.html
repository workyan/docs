About the app

KidsDose-Tracker helps parents and caregivers keep a clear medicine record for each child. You can log medicine name, amount, time, and reminders, then review recent history and share a care summary when handing off to another caregiver.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?
No. The current version has no sign-in, registration, or subscription flow.

Is my data uploaded to a server?
No. In the current version, child profiles, presets, and logs are stored locally on your device.

How do reminders work?
Reminder timing is based on the interval you enter for each medicine preset or manual log. Always follow the medicine label and your clinician's instructions.

Does the app tell me safe dosage amounts?
No. KidsDose-Tracker is a record-keeping tool only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or dosage recommendations.

App Review Notes

The app does not require a test account and does not depend on a backend service. Reviewers can open the app and directly test child profiles, medicine presets, logging flows, reminder setup, history review, and summary sharing.
