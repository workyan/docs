About the app

Contraction-Watch helps you time labor contractions from Apple Watch with one tap, then review duration, interval, and recent timing history on iPhone. You can also export a clean contraction log for your partner, doula, or care team.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?
No. The current version does not require sign-in, registration, or subscription.

Does the app work with Apple Watch?
Yes. The app includes an Apple Watch companion for quick start and stop timing from your wrist.

Is my data uploaded to a server?
No. In the current version, contraction logs and settings are stored locally on your device.

Can I export my contraction log?
Yes. The app includes an export flow so you can share a clean timing summary when you choose.

Does the app provide medical advice?
No. Contraction-Watch is a timing and logging tool only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

App Review Notes

The app does not require a test account and does not depend on a backend service. Reviewers can open the app and directly test Apple Watch timing, iPhone history review, and export flows.
