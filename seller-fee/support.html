Seller-Fee Support

Support page for the Seller-Fee iPhone app.

Seller-Fee helps marketplace sellers estimate fees, net profit, margin, and target sale price in seconds.

Contact support

For help, bug reports, or account-related questions about your App Store purchase, email 4532@qq.com.

When contacting support

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful when a calculation or layout looks incorrect.

Frequently asked questions

Does Seller-Fee require an account?
No. The app is designed to work without login.

Does Seller-Fee upload my data?
No. The current version stores calculations and saved history on your device.

Is Seller-Fee affiliated with Etsy?
No. Seller-Fee is an independent calculator app for sellers.
