FlightParamedic Prep

Support

A focused iPhone study companion for flight paramedic certification preparation, with exam-style questions, rationales, and a study plan built around your target date.

Getting started

Choose your target exam date, work through questions by topic, and review the rationale after each answer. Your progress and study preferences are saved locally on your device.

Common questions

Is this app a source of clinical guidance?

No. FlightParamedic Prep is for education and exam preparation only. It does not provide patient-specific advice or replace local protocols, medical direction, or formal training.

Where is my progress stored?

Your study progress, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account or upload your study data to a developer-operated server.

How do I restore a Pro purchase?

Use the Restore Purchases option in the app. Restores must use the same Apple ID that made the original purchase. Apple manages purchase eligibility and billing.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID: open Settings, select your name, then Subscriptions. Cancellations and billing changes are handled by Apple.

How do I remove my study data?

Reset or delete data within the app when that option is available. Removing the app also removes locally stored app data, subject to your device and backup settings.

Need more help?

Please contact the developer using the contact details listed on the App Store page. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue so we can help efficiently.

For urgent clinical questions, follow your local protocols and contact the appropriate clinical or emergency resource. This app cannot provide medical direction.
