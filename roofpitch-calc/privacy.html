RoofPitch-Calc Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026

RoofPitch-Calc is an iPhone utility for calculating roof pitch, slope angle, rafter length, roof area, and squares. This policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

RoofPitch-Calc does not require account creation, does not use advertising trackers, and is designed so calculation history, presets, and settings stay on your device.

Information the app handles

When you use RoofPitch-Calc, you may enter measurement and calculation values such as rise, run, span, overhang, roof dimensions, pitch presets, unit preferences, and saved jobsite presets. The app may also store your recent calculations and last-used settings for convenience.

How information is used

This information is used only to perform roof math, display results, restore your preferences, and make repeated calculations faster on the same device.

Data storage

RoofPitch-Calc is designed for offline use. Calculation inputs, saved presets, recent history, and settings are stored locally on your device. The current version does not provide account registration, cloud sync, or a remote project database.

What RoofPitch-Calc does not do

  • Does not require login for core features.
  • Does not sell your data.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Does not include third-party ad SDKs.
  • Does not collect payment card details inside the app.

Third-party services

The app may rely on standard Apple platform services that support installation, App Store purchase validation, and user-initiated share actions. If a future version adds analytics, cloud backup, or other external integrations, this page will be updated before those changes are introduced.

Your choices

You can clear saved values and recent calculations from within the app where supported, or remove local app data by deleting the app from your device.

Children's privacy

RoofPitch-Calc is a construction utility and is not directed to children.

Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the developer contact information listed on the App Store product page or visit the support page.
