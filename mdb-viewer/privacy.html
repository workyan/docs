Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

MDB-Viewer is a paid iPhone utility for opening common .mdb and .accdb table data. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data Collection

MDB-Viewer does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Files You Open

Database files that you import or open are processed on your device for viewing table data. MDB-Viewer does not upload your database files, table names, rows, exported CSV files, or file contents to our servers.

Local Storage

The app may use local device storage only as needed to open files, keep temporary working data, or support normal app functionality. You can delete app data by removing files you imported or by deleting the app from your device.

Network Use

MDB-Viewer is designed as an offline viewer. It does not use network services to process your database files.

Children's Privacy

MDB-Viewer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Microsoft Access Compatibility

MDB-Viewer is an unofficial compatibility utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft. Some files, encrypted databases, forms, reports, macros, and complex fields may be unsupported.

Policy Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, please use the MDB-Viewer support page.
