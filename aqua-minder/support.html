Support

Help with water tests, care history, reminders, and exports.

Aqua-Minder helps freshwater aquarium keepers record water parameters, maintenance, feeding, and treatment history in one simple timeline.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Aqua-Minder works without registration or sign-in. Your records are stored locally on your device.

How do I log a water test or care activity?

Open the Log tab, choose Water test, Maintenance, Feeding, or Treatment, enter the details, and save. The new record will appear in your tank timeline.

Why is a trend not showing?

A trend needs at least two saved water tests containing a reading for the selected parameter. Add another water test, then return to Trends.

How do reminders work?

Care tasks appear in the Reminders screen. Complete a matching water test or maintenance record to advance its due date. If notification reminders are available, also confirm that iOS notification permission is enabled.

How do I export my aquarium history?

Open More and choose Export history. Aqua-Minder prepares a CSV file and opens the iOS share sheet so you can save or send it to a destination you choose.

How do I delete my data?

Open More and use Delete tank. This removes the tank, its records, and reminders from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes locally stored app data.

Does Aqua-Minder provide treatment advice?

No. Treatment entries are records only. Aqua-Minder does not diagnose fish disease or recommend medication. Follow product labels and consult a qualified aquatic veterinarian when needed.

Contact support

Use the developer contact information listed on Aqua-Minder's App Store page. To help us understand a problem, include your device model, iOS version, app version, what you expected, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

App Review information

Aqua-Minder does not require a test account or a backend service. Reviewers can launch the app, create a tank, add sample records, inspect trends, manage care tasks, and test CSV export without signing in.
