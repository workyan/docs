FlightParamedic Prep

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

FlightParamedic Prep is an educational study tool. Your study progress and preferences stay on your device. The app does not use advertising, analytics, accounts, or a developer-operated server to collect your information.

Information the app stores

The app stores information needed for its study features locally on your device, such as your selected study settings, question progress, scores, and planned exam date. This information is not transmitted to us by the app.

How information is used

Local information is used only to provide and personalize the app’s study experience, including showing your progress and helping you organize your preparation plan. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you choose a Pro subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card number or other payment credentials. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Sharing

FlightParamedic Prep does not sell, rent, disclose, or share your study information with third parties. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored study information remains on your device until you reset or delete it in the app, where available, or remove the app. Deleting the app removes data in its local app container, subject to your device, backup, and operating-system settings.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because it does not provide user accounts or transmit personal information to us, we do not maintain user profiles.

Educational use only

FlightParamedic Prep supports exam preparation and self-study only. It is not medical advice, clinical decision support, or a substitute for local protocols, medical direction, or formal training.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The current version will always be posted here with its effective date.

Contact and support

For privacy questions or support, use the developer contact details on the App Store listing, or visit the FlightParamedic Prep Support page.
