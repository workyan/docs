Privacy Policy

CrossStitch-Viewer

Effective date: July 2, 2026

CrossStitch-Viewer is an iPhone app for opening cross stitch patterns, marking stitched areas, and saving progress locally.

Summary: CrossStitch-Viewer is designed to work without an account, without advertising SDKs, and without cross-app tracking. Pattern files and project progress are intended to stay on your device.

Information the app handles

The app may handle the following information to provide its core features:

  • Pattern files you choose to import from Files or Photos.
  • Project progress, stitch markings, notes, and viewer position.
  • Basic local app settings needed to reopen projects where you left off.

How information is used

The information above is used only to display your patterns, restore your in-progress work, and support the app's on-device project management features.

Data storage

CrossStitch-Viewer is intended to store imported patterns, progress, and notes locally on your device. The current version does not require account creation and does not rely on a dedicated developer-operated backend for core use.

Tracking and advertising

  • No cross-app tracking is intended.
  • No third-party advertising SDK is intended.
  • No sale of personal information is intended.

Permissions

If you import a pattern from Files or Photos, the app may access only the specific file or photo you choose. The app does not require a user account to function.

Your choices

You can remove imported projects and related local progress from within the app. You can also delete the app from your device to remove locally stored app data managed by iOS.

Children's privacy

CrossStitch-Viewer is a hobby productivity app and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

If the app's data practices change in a future release, this policy will be updated and the new version will be posted at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions about CrossStitch-Viewer, use the developer contact information listed on the App Store product page or visit the support page.
