Golf-Stroke Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Golf-Stroke is a simple golf scorekeeping app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what information the app handles, where that information is stored, and what the current version of the app does not collect.

Summary: the current version has no account system, no ads, no third-party analytics, and no cross-app tracking. Round history, score data, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Information the App Stores

To provide scorekeeping features, Golf-Stroke may store the following information on your device:

  • Round history, including hole-by-hole scores and penalty strokes.
  • App settings and preferences, such as sync-related options.
  • Basic local state needed to restore the last used session and screen flow.

Where Data Is Stored

The current version stores app data locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Golf-Stroke does not require account registration and does not upload round data to a developer-operated server.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, or mailing address.
  • Location data, contacts, photos, microphone input, or camera input.
  • Advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising.
  • Third-party analytics or cross-app tracking data.

Payments

If you purchase the app through the App Store, payment processing is handled by Apple. Golf-Stroke does not collect or store your payment card information inside the app.

Third-Party Services

As of this policy date, Golf-Stroke does not include third-party ad SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or a cloud account system. If a future version changes the app's data handling practices, this policy will be updated before or when those changes are released.

How to Remove Local Data

You can remove locally stored data by deleting saved rounds inside the app when that option is available, or by uninstalling the app from your device. Because the current version does not use cloud accounts, deleted local data may not be recoverable.

Children's Privacy

Golf-Stroke is a general-purpose scorekeeping utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

If the app's privacy practices materially change, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
