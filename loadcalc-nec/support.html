LoadCalc-NEC Support

This page is provided for the App Store support link and review reference.

LoadCalc-NEC is a fast iPhone utility for NEC-oriented service, feeder, and branch load calculations. It is built for electricians, apprentices, inspectors, and serious DIY users who need quick electrical load math without a spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

Does LoadCalc-NEC require an account?
No. The app is designed to work without login.

Does LoadCalc-NEC upload my calculations?
No. The current version is designed for offline use and stores saved jobs and recent calculation data on your device.

Can I use LoadCalc-NEC without network access?
Yes. Core calculations are intended to work offline.

What can the app calculate?
It supports NEC-oriented residential service, feeder, and common branch load scenarios.
