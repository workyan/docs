PipeSlope-Calc Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

PipeSlope-Calc is an iPhone utility for calculating pipe slope, pitch, angle, drop, and common field conversions. This policy explains how the app handles data.

1. Core privacy position

PipeSlope-Calc is designed for fast field use without requiring account creation or sign-in for core calculator features.

2. Information you enter

When you use the app, you may enter values such as run, drop, slope presets, unit preferences, and calculation notes or recent history. These values are used only to perform calculations and show results inside the app.

3. Local storage

Recent calculations, presets, and settings may be stored locally on your device so the app can restore your last-used inputs and preferences. The current version is intended to keep this data on-device.

4. Data collection and sharing

PipeSlope-Calc is not designed to sell your data, rent your data, or track you across apps and websites. The current version is also not intended to require a cloud account for basic use.

5. Third-party services

The app may rely on standard Apple platform services related to App Store distribution, purchase handling, crash delivery by the operating system, and user-initiated sharing features. If future versions add analytics, cloud sync, or other external services, this policy will be updated before those practices change.

6. Your choices

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing saved values where supported in the app or by deleting the app from your device.

7. Children's privacy

PipeSlope-Calc is a trade utility and is not directed to children.

8. Policy updates

This page may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be posted here with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, visit the support page for the current support contact path.
