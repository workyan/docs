Rental-Report helps landlords, tenants, and hosts create room-by-room inspection reports with photos, notes, signatures, and PDF export. Use this page for support links, common questions, and App Store review metadata.

Common Questions

1. Does the app require an account?

No. The current product scope is designed without account registration or a required cloud login.

2. Where are my reports stored?

The app is designed for local-first use, with report data kept on the device unless you choose to export or share it.

3. How do I share a report?

Generate a PDF export in the app and use the iOS share sheet to send it by email, message, Files, or another destination you choose.
