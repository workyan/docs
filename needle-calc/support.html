Needle-Calc: Needlepoint Support

Support information for Needle-Calc: Needlepoint.

About the app

Needle-Calc helps needlepoint stitchers plan a project before stitching. It estimates canvas cuts, stitch counts, thread quantities, per-color needs, and a practical shopping list.

Frequently asked questions

Are the thread and stitch results exact?
No. Results are planning estimates. Canvas, stitch choice, thread coverage, tension, waste, and personal technique can affect the final amount needed.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. The app is designed for offline use and does not require sign-in or account creation.

Where are my saved projects stored?
Projects, color plans, and settings are stored locally on your device. The current version does not provide cloud sync or recovery.

Can I share a shopping list?
Yes. Select the share option in the app to send generated text through the Apple system share sheet to a destination you choose.

My project is missing. What can I do?
Check whether it was deleted or whether app data was cleared. Because data remains on-device, it cannot be restored from a developer server.

Feedback and bug reports

For help or to report a problem, use the developer contact shown on the App Store listing. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue when possible.

App Review notes

The app has no login, test account, or backend dependency. Reviewers can immediately test canvas calculations, thread estimates, color planning, saved projects, and shopping-list sharing.
