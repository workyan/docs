DeployPay-US Support

DeployPay-US helps users estimate military pay, deployment pay, BAH, BAS, and drill pay quickly. If the app is not working as expected, use the links below to send a support request.

Open a support request View privacy policy

Common Questions

  • Numbers look different from official pay: confirm the app year, assumptions, and current official tables.
  • BAH estimate looks off: verify the selected ZIP code or location and check whether official rates changed.
  • Deployment pay seems incomplete: review combat zone, family separation, and other scenario inputs.
  • Saved scenarios missing: confirm whether app data was cleared or the device was restored.

Support Covers

  • App launch or stability problems
  • Calculator input or output issues
  • Questions about pay assumptions shown in the app
  • Privacy-related questions

Include These Details

  • Device model and iOS version
  • App version
  • Calculator type involved, such as monthly pay or deployment pay
  • The inputs you used and the result you expected
  • Screenshots if the problem is visible on screen
