Frequently asked questions

Does Turnover Checklist require an account?
No. The app is designed for local solo use and does not require login.

Does the app upload property or checklist data?
No. The current version stores properties, turnover sessions, notes, restock status, and photo records locally on your device.

Can I use it without network access?
Yes. Core turnover workflows are intended to work offline.

What can I export?
You can export a completion record that summarizes completed tasks, skipped items, issue notes, restock status, and an optional photo summary.

What if camera access is denied?
You can still run the checklist with text tasks and notes. Photo proof is optional unless you choose to use it.
