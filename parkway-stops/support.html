What Parkway Stops does

Parkway Stops is an offline-ready iPhone guide to worthwhile stops along the Blue Ridge Parkway. Browse by milepost, category, facilities, and travel direction—even where your connection is limited.

Common topics

  • Guide content, search, filters, or stop details.
  • Favorites that do not appear as expected.
  • Opening a destination in Apple Maps.
  • Suggestions for a stop, correction, or missing practical detail.

Before you write

  • Close and reopen the app, then try the action again.
  • Check the App Store for the latest version.
  • For a content issue, note the stop name and milepost if available.

Include these details

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The Parkway Stops app version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if the issue is visible on screen.

Contact support

Please open an issue in our support tracker. We aim to review requests within three business days.

Road, trail, weather, and facility conditions can change quickly. Confirm important details with official sources before you travel.
