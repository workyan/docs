CrossStitch-Viewer

CrossStitch-Viewer is a focused iPhone app for opening cross stitch patterns, marking stitched areas, and resuming work exactly where you stopped.

Common questions

Does the app require an account?
No. The app is designed for local use without sign-in.

What pattern types can I open?
The app is intended for common cross stitch pattern files, including image-based charts and supported document imports.

Where is my progress saved?
Your project progress and notes are intended to be saved on your device so you can reopen a project later.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure the pattern file finished importing before closing the app.
  • Confirm the file is accessible in Files or Photos on your device.
  • Update to the latest App Store version if a problem persists.
  • Restart the app and reopen the project if a chart does not render as expected.

Contacting support

For bug reports, purchase-related questions, or general support, use the developer contact information listed on the App Store product page.

When contacting support, include your device model, iOS version, app version, the steps to reproduce the issue, and screenshots if available.

App Review information

CrossStitch-Viewer does not require a test account for review. The app is intended to work without login, subscription, or external account setup in version 1.
