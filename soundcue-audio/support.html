Common questions

  • My audio does not appear. Reopen the file from the Files app or share it to SoundCue, then confirm that the file is still available on your device.
  • I cannot find a marker. Open the audio item and use search or tags to locate the saved note or timestamp.
  • I am changing devices. Export your markers before deleting the app or moving to another device.
  • Playback stops unexpectedly. Check that the original audio file has not been moved, renamed, or removed from Files.

When contacting support

Please include your device model, iOS version, SoundCue version, a short description of what happened, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful when the issue is visible on screen. Do not include private audio files unless specifically requested.

For questions about how SoundCue handles data, please review the Privacy Policy or submit a support request.
